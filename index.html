About this prototype

Two groups are simulated in this browser. Villagers follow traversable routes, and roads speed up automated work. Wheat, flour and food now have limited village storage and local building inventories. Progress saves locally. Real Telegram multiplayer comes later. No bot credentials are needed to play this demo.
