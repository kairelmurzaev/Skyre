# Skyre product review: Lessons from Clash of Clans (November 18, 2026)

## Scope

This is a detailed product-design study of the documented Clash of Clans
features, official Supercell game and support materials, October 2026
interface overhaul, and public player feedback. It is not a claim to have
completed every in-game level or to access proprietary game systems.

## Full feature taxonomy

- **Home Village:** angled village view, placement and rearrangement,
  Town Hall levels, resource collection, collectors, storage, defenses,
  building upgrades, builders, upgrade timers, and new unlocks.
- **Combat:** army training and deployment, PvP village attacks,
  defenses, regular and ranked modes, battle logs and replays,
  tactical experimentation and changing troop/defense balance.
- **Additional locations:** Builder Base as a distinct progression and
  battle environment; Clan Capital as collaborative construction and
  raid gameplay.
- **Character progression:** Heroes, Equipment, Pets, research and
  troop upgrades, cosmetics and skin/scenery customization.
- **Social and events:** Clans, donations, Clan Wars and War Leagues,
  Clan Capital projects, leaderboards, season challenges, limited-time
  events, co-op objectives, and periodic live content.
- **Economy:** resources and unlock paths; timers, builders and
  trade-offs support long-term planning, with optional purchases.
- **Feedback:** clearly labeled prices, action statuses and timers,
  distinct silhouettes, colorful resource indicators, progress
  animation and contextual object commands.
- **Mobile presentation:** relatively uncluttered world at center,
  large buttons at the edges, short panels with live game behind them,
  clear place/rotate/confirm/cancel interactions, responsive camera.

## The important 2026 UI lesson

Supercell announced an extensive UI rebuild October 8, 2026. Its old
interface accumulated too many unrelated buttons as equipment, pets,
magic items, Clan Capital and events were added. The new Build menu
centralizes upgrades with sorting, filtering and camera focus. Battle
is a separate top-level entry. Panels display only relevant resources
and contextual states, and introductory mini-tutorials teach key
changes.

In an October 15 follow-up, Supercell acknowledged friction: extra
taps to common tools, filters resetting, HUD scaling issues, and
unclear location of some functions. Therefore Skyre should preserve
quick access to frequently used actions instead of burying them
behind several menus.

## Translation into original Skyre gameplay

| Clash principle | Skyre adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Readable angled village | Keep the diorama island and physical desk, improve camera and silhouettes |
| Contextual building controls | Tap a desk prop to reveal Move / Rotate / Store |
| Visual placement preview | Red/green ghost, touch-drag cursor, explicit Build / Cancel |
| Distinct resources | Show wood and stone only during desk construction |
| Layout editing and saving | Half-unit placement, move without repurchase, protective collision rules; later undo/layout presets |
| Upgrade and collection loop | Build and expand, then craft, garden, decorate, collect and explore |
| Distinct progression paths | Builder, explorer, merchant and town-life activities; no mandatory combat |
| Clans and social play | Optional collaborative desk projects and island visits later |
| Builder Base / Clan Capital | Different desk environments and group-scale expeditions later |
| Heroes / Pets | Unique residents, skills, personalities and animal companions later |
| Events and seasons | Short village festivals, traveling traders, weather surprises and collectibles later |

## Skyre functionality gap assessment

**Already implemented:** 3D world and desk; resource management;
farming, automation and routing; building upgrades and goals;
desk discovery, mug/plant activities and short book expeditions;
mobile app shell and persistent worlds. V1.5 adds movable free
desk props and contextual editing.

**Needs improvement next:** richer environmental interaction,
finer in-island prop placement, better visual feedback and animation,
saved object layouts, undo/redo history, more meaningful reasons to
interact with structures, NPC individuality, skillful mini-games,
shared/community activities and a broader non-farming progression.

**Do not copy literally:** copyrighted art, heroes, troops, music,
the raid loop, Shop layout or graphics; premium-currency pressure,
long enforced waiting and competitive combat are not required for
Skyre's creative sandbox purpose.

## Roadmap

1. **Interactions + decoration:** richer reusable object inventory,
   island furniture editing, undo, and satisfying action feedback.
2. **Living desk:** additional desk landmarks, environmental puzzles,
   expeditions, villagers doing independent activities and hobbies.
3. **Activity tracks:** fishing, collecting, shopkeeping, decorating,
   photography and crafting, each with optional milestones.
4. **Social:** friend visits, co-op projects, friendly mini-challenges.
5. **Personalization:** saved layouts, desk themes, terrain freedom,
   accessibility options and performance tuning for large islands.
